Privacy policy

Snore Recorder · last updated 5 October 2026

What the app does

Snore Recorder is a personal Android app, not sold or advertised, used by one family. It records audio at night, detects snoring and possible breathing pauses on the phone, and builds a report.

Data on the phone

  • Night audio recordings, detected events, nightly summaries, optional sleep-quality and blood-pressure notes, and a diagnostic log.
  • All of this stays in the app's storage on the phone. Old audio is deleted automatically after a set number of nights.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and sends no data to the developer.

Google Drive (optional)

  • If the phone owner connects a Google account, the app uploads small results to that person's own Google Drive: a text summary, a PDF report and short audio clips (about 10 seconds) of notable moments. Full-night recordings are not uploaded.
  • The app uses only the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files and folder it created itself, nothing else in the Drive.
  • The app may share its own results folder with one family member whose email address the owner enters in the app.
  • No one else, including the developer, receives this data.

Google API Services

Snore Recorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to upload and share the app's own results as described above; it is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Deleting data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the phone.
  • Uploaded results can be deleted in Google Drive at any time; the app's Drive access can be removed in the Google account settings (Security → Third-party connections).
