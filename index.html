Snore Recorder

A private Android app for one family.

Snore Recorder records sound at night on an Android phone, counts snoring and possible breathing pauses on the phone itself, and prepares a simple report for a doctor. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea.

Optionally, it uploads small results (a text summary, a PDF report and short audio clips) to the phone owner's own Google Drive, so a family member can see them.
